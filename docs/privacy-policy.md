# DrishtiNav — Privacy Policy (draft for Play Store listing)

_Last updated: 2026-09-29. Replace the contact line before publishing._

## What DrishtiNav does with your data

DrishtiNav is an assistive navigation aid. It is designed to keep your
data on your device.

### Data that never leaves your phone

- **Camera frames.** Processed in real time for obstacle detection and
  depth sensing. Frames are never stored, never uploaded, never shared.
- **Saved places.** Names and coordinates you save are stored as a file
  in the app's private storage, on your device only.
- **Settings.** Speech rate, alert distances, and preferences are stored
  in the app's private preferences, on your device only.

### Data sent to third-party services (only when you use navigation)

- **Route planning:** when you start navigation, your current location
  and destination coordinates are sent to the public OSRM demo server
  (`router.project-osrm.org`) to compute a walking route. See
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.
- **Destination search:** when you search for a place by name, the search
  text is sent to Nominatim (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`). No account,
  no identifier is attached beyond a generic app User-Agent.

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no accounts, no sign-in. We do not
sell data because we do not collect it.

### Permissions and why

- **Camera** — obstacle detection and depth sensing. Required.
- **Location (fine)** — turn-by-turn navigation guidance. Only used while
  navigating; only for routing and guidance.
- **Vibration** — haptic obstacle alerts.
- **Internet** — route planning and place search only. Obstacle scanning
  works fully offline.
